Three steps. No refunds.

Pay the charity

To our stakeholdersOffice of the Chief Executor

Terms & Damnations is building the world's first for-zero-profit streamer corporation.

The market opportunity is clear: people want things, and most of them will settle for less than they asked for. We close that gap. Clients name their terms, pay their price directly to a registered charity, and receive custom software, built live, that fulfills the contract in full, as interpreted by management.

Our model is simple. Every dollar we earn is donated. Our stakeholders help decide where it goes. Our margins are, and will remain, zero.

We are a startup. We are growing. We are accepting new employees at every level of the organization.
