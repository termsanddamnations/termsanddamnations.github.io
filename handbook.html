§1

Welcome to Terms & Damnations

Our Chief Executor Officer is The Executor, a stick figure who is also the Devil. Our Legal Department is also The Executor. Questions about this structure may be directed to HR.

Mission statement: to deliver software at scale, with integrity, as interpreted by management, and give away 100% of the proceeds.

  • Every stream is a business day. Meetings are held with complete seriousness, and a sprint is four streams.
  • Employees climb the org chart. Showing up earns Brimstone and titles; subscriptions come with management titles. Your title is your influence.
  • Stakeholders decide where the money goes. Every month the Two Chambers of Hell vote on the donations.
  • Clients sign contracts with The Executor. Proposals and deals move across the public Agility board, from backlog to acceptance.

Terms and damnations may apply.

The Agility board

Every item moves through eight stages, each named after a phase of the software development lifecycle.

  1. Product Backlog (intake): submitted, awaiting the attention of management.
  2. Funnel (requirements): The Executor drafts the terms.
  3. Committed (sign-off): the client signs with !sign. For deals, the donation is confirmed and a contract number such as TD-0001 is issued.
  4. In Progress (implementation): built live on stream. WIP limit: 1, since there is one employee.
  5. Review (QA and UAT): demonstrated on stream while chat performs QA.
  6. Done (deployment): delivered, as interpreted by management.
  7. Accepted (maintenance): the client accepts with !accept. Silence for seven days after delivery constitutes acceptance. Maintenance is not included.
  8. Damned (won't fix): rejected by Legal, with the reason read aloud.

Upvotes. Hellspawn and above can !vote any item at any stage, at their rank's weight. Upvotes show what employees want done; they move no money.

The Garbage Clause

The Devil interprets every wish creatively. You agree that whatever you receive fulfills the contract in full, however literal, useless or cursed it is.

No refunds. Your payment is a donation to a registered charity, not a purchase. The Company never holds it, so it cannot return it.

Serious deals. Now and then a request is real and doable. Then the Devil negotiates a fair price and the deal moves off stream with a written agreement. Serious deals are for adults (18+) only.

Legal will reject any deal involving

  • anything illegal, or harm to anyone
  • personal information about real people
  • cheating, or breaking another platform's rules
  • malware, hacking or security work
  • schoolwork you are supposed to do yourself

Under 18? Ask a parent before you donate anything.

§5

The Two Chambers of Hell

Every month the donation pool is split in half. One half is divided by vote share; the other half funds the top three of five charities The Executor picks. Devil deal money never enters the pool; it goes straight to each deal's paired charity.

  • Who votes: every employee from Hellspawn up, in both Chambers, at their rank's vote weight.
  • Chamber of Souls: a standing ballot of three to five approved charities. Each gets the share of its half that matches its share of the vote.
  • Chamber of the Devil: The Executor picks five charities each month, and the three with the most votes are funded.
  • When: ballots open at the first stream of the month and close on screen at the last one. You may change your vote until the close; your last vote counts.
  • Ties: The Executor breaks them.
  • Payout: donations go out within a week of the close, and receipts appear at the next earnings call.
  • Legal's veto: an option is pulled if the charity turns out not to be in good standing.

Live results are in the public disclosures.

Meeting etiquette

Meetings are run with total seriousness, regardless of content.

  • Never break character in a meeting. Nod. Take notes. Ask follow-up questions.
  • Use the vocabulary: synergy, alignment, stakeholders, deliverables, circle back, quarter over quarter.
  • Report numbers with confidence. "This sprint we shipped 0.4 features, a 300% increase quarter over quarter."
  • Blockers are real objects. "Yesterday I aligned cross-functionally with the chair. Today I'm leveraging synergies with the microwave. Blockers: the chair."
  • The profit margin is always 0%, and The Executor is always proud of it.
§7

Code of conduct

The one rule that ends employment immediately: never try to identify The Executor. The Executor is a stick figure, and that is all anyone needs to know. No guessing, no digging through clues, no posting theories. "I think I know who you are" counts too.

Everyone is also expected to:

  • Keep everyone's personal information private, including your own.
  • Skip hate, slurs and harassment. Jokes about the Company are encouraged; jokes at a real person's expense are not.
  • Treat text-to-speech as a privilege. No slurs, no personal info, no spam. HR can skip any message.
  • Not spam or self-promote without asking HR first.
  • Follow the platform's rules. The Company operates on someone else's property.

HR (the moderators) enforces this in three steps:

StepCorporate termWhat happens
1Written upA warning in chat
2Unpaid leaveA timeout
3TerminatedA permanent ban

Serious offenses, such as hate speech or trying to identify The Executor, go straight to step 3. HR's decisions are final.

§8

Transparency and fine print

Every dollar is shown publicly, so nobody has to take the Devil's word for anything.

  • The quarterly earnings call shows income by source, the platform's cut, each month's vote results, and the donation receipts.
  • The Devil's Ledger lists every deal: the client's username, the terms, the amount, the charity, the donation link, and what was delivered.

The fine print

  • A subscription is a purchase, not a donation. It is not tax-deductible for you. The Company donates its earnings after the platform's cut.
  • Deal payments are donations you make directly to the charity. The Company never touches that money.
  • Brimstone has no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out.
  • "Shareholder" and "stakeholder" are job titles, not ownership. Nobody owns any part of the Company or gets a return.
  • Terms & Damnations is not a registered nonprofit. It is a for-zero-profit joke with real donations. Every charity on a ballot is a registered nonprofit that Legal has checked.
  • All software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express, implied, or infernal.
§9

Where we operate

The Company streams on YouTube and Twitch at the same time.

  • YouTube is headquarters. Every stream, meeting and earnings call stays up there as a permanent replay.
  • Twitch is the branch office. Chat commands, Brimstone and paid ranks work there.

YouTube memberships will become paid ranks once the channel qualifies for them. Until then, paid ranks are Twitch subscriptions only, and YouTube viewers are welcome to visit the branch office.
